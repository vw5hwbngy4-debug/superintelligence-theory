Concepts / working vocabulary

Words for the costs
that remain.

The concepts are tools for asking better questions about AI abundance. They are not all measured quantities, and they are not interchangeable. This page favors short definitions, useful distinctions and routes back to the original documents.

↗
Economics / design

Affordable variation

Distinct possibilities should be cheap enough not just to generate, but to compare, enter, abandon, revisit and preserve. A thousand nearly identical outputs do not necessarily buy a thousand meaningful alternatives.

Primary synthesis: Beyond Abundance.
↶
Systems property

Reversibility

The practical ability to undo, exit, fork, recover or change direction without losing disproportionate amounts of context, data, relationships or accumulated work. Cheap entry with expensive exit is an incomplete freedom.

Related: Reversible Stress & ΔR; Conduits of Intelligence.
≈
Field condition

Coherence / slop

Coherence is the capacity to integrate, sort, preserve, transform or discard abundant material while keeping a field usable. Slop is material or a visible signature of failed integration, not a symmetrical opposite of coherence. A coherent field can contain slop.

September 2026 interpretive site revision; do not project this definition back into every older paper.

Power architecture

Conduits of intelligence

The entry routes by which intelligence becomes useful or influential: payments, compute, devices, runtime, identity, context, recommendations, social graphs and trust. Their design affects agency, permissions, portability and lock-in.

Institutions

Institutional softening

A proposed transition in which institutions preserve coordinating function while reducing rigidity, irreversible commitments and unnecessary coercive or administrative pressure. It is an architectural thesis, not a finding that such transitions have occurred universally.

Comparative research

Three independent axes

Capability acceleration, civilizational viability and power architecture are different questions. Growing machine capability alone does not settle who controls infrastructure or whether people can absorb the rate of change.

Creative circulation

Memetic manifestation

AI can shorten the path from noticing another person's artifact to expressing “this, but…”. Remixing can open genuinely different branches, or selection pressure can drive endless near-duplicates. The medium's design influences what gets preserved.

Information ecology

Reflexive informational climate

Model-assisted outputs enter public language, products, criticism, tests and documentation. Later systems may encounter parts of that altered environment through data, evaluations or retrieval. This does not mean a deployed model's weights change whenever it reads a webpage.

Distinguishes direct model adaptation from broader ecosystem feedback.
A useful warning

A name is not proof.

A concept becomes useful when it distinguishes things clearly, points to observations that might challenge it and stays connected to the material it was meant to explain. Where the corpus moves into thermodynamic or cosmological analogy, the analogy is not treated here as established physical science.
