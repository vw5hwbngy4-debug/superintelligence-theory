03 / Two playable games

Hatch a worm.
Feed a snake.
Explore both.

Two separate games · playable nowSlopWorm World · browser microgames

Hatch.
Limitbreak.
Explore.

SlopWorm World is the explorable world at slopworm.com. Hatch Starworm from a cosmic egg, steer through space and ocean, and discover strange creatures with games inside them. The original SlopSnake arcade is a separate game at slopsnake.com: catch and beat microgames to grow your snake.

SlopWorm is the world and creature. Starworm is its hero; Rainbow Starworm is the Limitbreak form. SlopSnake is the separate game where the snake grows by eating games.
