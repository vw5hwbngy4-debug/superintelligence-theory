01 / The comparative frame

Three axes,
not one progress curve.

The August 2026 paper compares capability-acceleration arguments with the Ambient Era's question of viability. It adds power architecture as a distinct variable rather than assuming more capability automatically means a more livable civilization.

A

Capability

How quickly effective AI, scientific and productive capability grows.

B

Viability

Whether people and institutions can absorb the change while retaining autonomy, attention and reversibility.

C

Power

Who sets the infrastructure, permissions and defaults through which capability becomes influence or support.

This three-axis synthesis is a comparative research architecture; the paper explicitly does not present the underlying frameworks as wholly empirically established.

02 / A companion architecture

The model race is visible.
The conduit race is structural.

ACTIVE CONDUITS

Import → process → export

A person deliberately takes in machine-assisted material, filters or edits it and turns it into papers, games, tools, decisions or culture.

PASSIVE CONDUITS

Absorb → transmit → assimilate

AI-shaped recommendations, defaults and interfaces can influence attention and behavior without an explicit chatbot interaction.

moneycomputehardwareruntimeidentitycontextdefaultssocialtrust

The question is not simply how intelligence is generated, but how it is routed, authorized, contextualized and made reversible. The companion page describes these as an entry stack and distinguishes closed conduits from reversible ones.

03 / From research to construction

An artifact is a beginning.
A field carries its consequences.

Cheap generation creates output. People, memory, relationships, constraints and the systems distributing that output determine whether it remains recoverable, remixable and meaningful. A feed is a conduit; a persistent social environment may become a field. Neither automatically guarantees coherence.

“Generative abundance changes the problem from producing information to maintaining coherence. ‘AI slop’ is the first folk recognition of that transition.”

Working formulation developed in the September 2026 site revisions. This is an interpretive thesis, not an empirical finding.

Slop is not the logical opposite of coherence. It can be output, residue or evidence of failed integration. Coherence describes a field's capacity to sort, retain, transform, fade or discard abundant material without losing continuity. A coherent field can contain slop.
