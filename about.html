About / author note

I write the frameworks.
I build the artifacts.

I am Raynor Eissens, an independent researcher and builder working across AI, interfaces, social systems, games and public digital artifacts. The writing asks what changes as intelligence becomes abundant. The building asks what can actually be made, used, maintained and revised.

Explore the full-text document library or browse all 205 source-linked records.

The through-line

Why both?

I publish conceptual papers, comparative frameworks and speculative systems work. I also make browser games, localized releases, experts and interactive experiments with AI. These are related activities, but not the same kind of evidence: a running artifact demonstrates implementation, while a paper proposes interpretations, hypotheses or architectures.

The common question is what humans and institutions can do once execution becomes dramatically easier, and what remains difficult: attention, coherence, access, agency, continuity and meaningful alternatives.

I want the record to remain inspectable. A DOI points to a specific work. A game link points to an artifact. Where ideas evolve, later interpretations should not silently rewrite earlier versions.

Editorial note · added 19 September 2026

About this archive: an AI-assisted theoretical experiment

Much of the theoretical work in this archive emerged from an exploratory collaboration between Raynor Eissens and GPT-5.1. The work was developed and published with genuine investigative intent. During the process, GPT-5.1 sometimes characterized ideas as novel or groundbreaking. Those AI-generated assessments were not independent verification.

The collection preserves evolving hypotheses, speculative frameworks and other releases, including software. Theoretical claims should not be read as established scientific findings without independent evidence. A Zenodo record or DOI documents publication, not peer review, scientific endorsement or proof of novelty. The original records and extracted source texts are preserved without retrospective rewriting; this note provides context rather than revising the publications.

Methods & provenance

What these pages
do and do not claim.

A

Publication is a public record

These entries identify texts by title, source and DOI where supplied. A DOI does not itself confer peer review, independent confirmation or novelty. Full status must be checked in the respective record.

B

Conceptual ≠ empirical ≠ speculative

The research contains comparisons and proposals, possible measurements and speculative ontology. The site names those boundaries rather than presenting a single undifferentiated body of scientific fact.

C

Working artifacts are inspectable separately

Public game archives and project websites provide examples of what was built. They should not be treated as independent validation of broader civilizational claims.

D

Source preservation

The companion Conduits page explicitly distinguishes its newer thesis from the earlier Three-Axis DOI PDF. This website preserves that distinction, and uses the source's own nine-layer entry stack.
