About this archive: an AI-assisted theoretical experiment

Much of the theoretical work in this archive emerged from an exploratory collaboration between Raynor Eissens and GPT-5.1. The work was developed and published with genuine investigative intent. During the process, GPT-5.1 sometimes characterized ideas as novel or groundbreaking. Those AI-generated assessments were not independent verification.

The collection preserves evolving hypotheses, speculative frameworks and other releases, including software. Theoretical claims should not be read as established scientific findings without independent evidence. A Zenodo record or DOI documents publication, not peer review, scientific endorsement or proof of novelty. The original records and extracted source texts are preserved without retrospective rewriting; this note provides context rather than revising the publications.
