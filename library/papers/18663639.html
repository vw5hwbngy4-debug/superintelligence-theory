Abstract

The Route Residue Operator (RR-1) defines the thermodynamic persistence of direction in

Ambient OS.

RR-1 establishes that routes do not exist as stored objects, memories, paths, or plans.

A route exists only as field residue created through embodied traversal.

Residue strengthens through use, weakens through non-use, and fades without deletion.

RR-1 formalizes navigation, reasoning, and pattern formation as resonant persistence, not

selection, optimization, or memory.

Route residue is not representation.

It is persistence.
