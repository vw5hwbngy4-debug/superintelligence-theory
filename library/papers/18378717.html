2. Definition

Semantic Boundary Law (SBL)

A system-level constraint that governs how meaning may transform within human–AI interaction.

Core principle:

Meaning may only be compressed, never expanded, without explicit human anchoring.

Compression includes:

• summarization

• abstraction

• contextual reduction

• prioritization

• deferral

Expansion includes:

• adding goals

• reframing values

• escalating intentions

• inventing new context

• introducing ungrounded interpretations

Only the human may authorize semantic expansion.

No AI system may introduce new semantic structures, goals, or interpretations without crossing
